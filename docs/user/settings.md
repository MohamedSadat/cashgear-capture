# Settings

Tray › **Settings…**. Changes apply with **OK** or **Apply**. **Reset to defaults** at the bottom resets the form; nothing is saved until OK.

## Output

What happens after a capture is a **workflow** of steps, in order, each on or off:
- open in the editor;
- save to the folder;
- copy;
- print;
- Word, PowerPoint or PDF;
- upload to a server or a service;
- open in an app;
- copy the link.

Recordings have a workflow of their own. Also on this tab: the capture folder, the image format, JPEG quality, and the file name template (`{yyyy}-{MM}-{dd}_{HH}-{mm}-{ss}`, `{counter}`, `{kind}`, `{window}`…).

## Destinations

The printer, Word and PowerPoint, PDF, FTP/FTPS/SFTP servers, HTTP uploads and other apps, each with a **Test** button. Passwords are encrypted for your Windows account and never kept in settings.json.

## Capture

- The delays (one for all captures, or one per kind).
- The capture toolbar at start-up.
- The pointer in captures, window shadows.
- Fixed-size and rounded regions, full-screen output, scrolling capture.
- HDR monitors.

## Recording

- Frame rate, quality and encoder.
- Sound: system audio, one app, the output device and the microphone.
- The microphone's clean-up, live meters and a microphone test.
- Evening out loudness.

## Overlays

Clicks, the pointer, keystrokes, the webcam and drawing on the screen ([Recording](recording.md#overlays)).

## Hotkeys

Click a box and press the keys. Each shows whether it works; a key another app owns is reported.
